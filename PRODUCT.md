# MFC Research Workbench
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The project owner, developing a small experimental MFC and a possible PhD application about a year after October 2026.

## Product Purpose
A local research tool connecting reactor geometry, historical observations, scientific evidence, and new experimental records.

## Stack
Implementation assumption: React, Vite, and Three.js; plain independent JavaScript calculation modules. The user requested a maintainable browser architecture and a real rendering library, without a specific framework.

## Operating Context
Start with 0.5–1 L bottle concepts, accessible graphite/carbon materials, two Notion pages, two Google documents, eight supplied images, and one shared AI conversation. Use at a desk and experimental bench.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Parametric bottle model; layer, cutaway and exploded controls; SI calculations; local notebook; exports; source register and uncertainties. Preserve the micro-scale prototype separately. Actual bottle measurements, cathode sealing, current collector material, separator properties, and measurement circuits remain undecided. No performance simulation or unsourced biological claims. Local-only delivery for this first version; no paid services.

## Evidence on Hand
See research/phase-0.md and public/sources. No CAD source exists in the supplied workspace. No confirmed historical time stamps, external load resistances, or replicate counts are available.

## Product Principles
- Every number has a unit and an evidence status.
- Preserve raw observations before interpretation.
- Geometry calculations do not predict biology.
- Test one change at a time with a measured circuit and controls.
- Keep data exportable and original materials intact.
